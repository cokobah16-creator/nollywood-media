# NACECA — Season 1 story canon (locked 7 Oct 2026)

These decisions are fixed. New missions, dialogue and art are written to fit them.

## The cast, as painted
- **Agent Kelechi** is a man, 26, the player character.
- **Osas Ehigie** is Mrs. Ehigie's son, a UNIBEN student.
- **The Voice**, the ransom negotiator, is a woman. Before the finale reveal, no line genders her.

## The Voice is Commander Adaeze
- **Motive.** She began shielding criminal contacts as intelligence sources, then came to depend on
  their money and influence. She calls it "keeping control."
- **Why Osas.** Osas found evidence that could expose her. The kidnapping is about silencing that evidence, not ransom.
- **Complexity.** Some of her earlier help to Kelechi was sincere. She values him, but expects loyalty
  to outrank the truth. The finale must let her make that case, not just be unmasked.
- **Plants already in the game.** Her sideways look at "I'll send word to the Anti-Kidnapping Squad" (M1, savvy path).
  More quiet plants are allowed in M1–M7 as long as a first-time player won't see the twist coming.

## Finale structure: one reveal, three approaches
Every route reaches Adaeze. The Mission 7 result sets the approach:

| M7 result | Finale approach |
|---|---|
| Held the cabinet (full fix) | Planned raid on the exact house in Ekosodin |
| Pulled out (partial trace) | Work the street on foot and tail the courier unseen |
| Power cut (no trace) | Trace the evening call live from the field office |

Earlier choices decide **how safely Osas is recovered** and **whether Adaeze's involvement can be proven**,
not whether the reveal happens.

## Allies who pay off
- **Tobi** (if rescued in M6) gives the testimony that ties the money to her.
- **KC** (if treated fairly in M2) helps locate or identify the courier.
- **Musa** (if he flipped in M4) supplies the "Engineer" tip that unlocks the alternate entrance.

## Season 2
Adaeze's defeat stands. The post-credits stinger reveals who protected her inside the wider system.
It sets up the next season without undoing this victory.

## Portrait use
- Before the reveal, the Voice's lines show a phone-call card, not a face.
- At the reveal, the Voice's lines switch to Adaeze's portraits.
- The separate "the_voice" portraits (a different woman) are not Adaeze. Recommended reuse: the courier
  who relays the calls, whom KC can help identify.

## Names (always)
- NACECA is the **National Anti-Corruption & Economic Crimes Agency**. Never "Anti-Cybercrime".
- The kidnapping unit is always written out as the **Anti-Kidnapping Squad** — in dialogue, speaker
  names, toasts, objectives, signs and news. Never "AKS" in anything a player sees. Code names
  (`aks()`, `o1_brief_aks`) can stay. "AKs" for rifles is a different word.
