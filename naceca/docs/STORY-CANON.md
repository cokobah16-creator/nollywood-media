# NACECA — Season 1 story canon (locked 7 Oct 2026)

These decisions are fixed. New missions, dialogue and art are written to fit them.

## The cast, as painted
- **Agent Kelechi** is a man, 26, the player character.
- **Osas Ehigie** is Mrs. Ehigie's son, a UNIBEN student.
- **The Voice**, the ransom negotiator, is a woman. Before the finale reveal, no line genders her.

## The Voice is Commander Adaeze
- **Motive.** She began shielding criminal contacts as intelligence sources, then came to depend on
  their money and influence. She calls it "keeping control."
- **Why Osas.** Osas found evidence that could expose her. The kidnapping is about silencing that evidence, not ransom.
- **Complexity.** Some of her earlier help to Kelechi was sincere. She values him, but expects loyalty
  to outrank the truth. The finale must let her make that case, not just be unmasked.
- **Plants already in the game.** Her sideways look at "I'll send word to the Anti-Kidnapping Squad" (M1, savvy path).
  More quiet plants are allowed in M1–M7 as long as a first-time player won't see the twist coming.

## Finale structure: one reveal, three approaches
Every route reaches Adaeze. The Mission 7 result sets the approach:

| M7 result | Finale approach |
|---|---|
| Held the cabinet (full fix) | Planned raid on the exact house in Ekosodin |
| Pulled out (partial trace) | Work the street on foot and tail the courier unseen |
| Power cut (no trace) | Trace the evening call live from the field office |

Earlier choices decide **how safely Osas is recovered** and **whether Adaeze's involvement can be proven**,
not whether the reveal happens.

## Allies who pay off
- **Tobi** (if rescued in M6) gives the testimony that ties the money to her.
- **KC** (if treated fairly in M2) helps locate or identify the courier.
- **Musa** (if he flipped in M4) supplies the "Engineer" tip that unlocks the alternate entrance.

## Season 2
Adaeze's defeat stands. The post-credits stinger reveals who protected her inside the wider system.
It sets up the next season without undoing this victory. Season 2 itself is recorded at the end of this file
("Season 2 — Operation Upstream").

## Portrait use
- Before the reveal, the Voice's lines show a phone-call card, not a face.
- At the reveal, the Voice's lines switch to Adaeze's portraits.
- The separate "the_voice" portraits (a different woman) are not Adaeze. Recommended reuse: the courier
  who relays the calls, whom KC can help identify.

## Names (always)
- NACECA is the **National Anti-Corruption & Economic Crimes Agency**. Never "Anti-Cybercrime".
- The kidnapping unit is always written out as the **Anti-Kidnapping Squad** — in dialogue, speaker
  names, toasts, objectives, signs and news. Never "AKS" in anything a player sees. Code names
  (`aks()`, `o1_brief_aks`) can stay. "AKs" for rifles is a different word.

## v13 additions (the investigation layer, decided 10 Oct 2026)
These extend the canon above; nothing here changes it. "Before M8" means before the finale reveal.

**The twist stays earned.** Before M8 no text, label, narrator, panel or toast names NACECA, the
Commander or her office as the source of a clue, and Senior Agent confirms nothing: the player draws
every link. Clues that may exist (each needs work the player chose to do): the "C.A." initials (beta);
a government car at Obi's gate, at Zuma Court and at the Foundation; a ledger page lost after the
player chose to send the ledger to her office (stated only after the reveal); the Engineer's calls to
"C." (only if the player pulled his line against her order).

- **Her tenure.** Adaeze has run the Lagos unit for more than ten years (Uche, Night Shift: "Eleven
  years. She signed my transfer to Lagos."). No v13 text gives a start date or ties anything to it.
- **"C."** is how the Voice is saved in KC's phone and in the warehouse burner, matching the courier's
  one saved number ("C") and the "C.A." payroll initials. "C." calls at seven. Never "CONTROL" (too close
  to her day-one line "I need control").
- **C.A. Consulting Services Ltd** — RC 1544870, incorporated 14 March 2019 (a registry fact, never tied
  to her); nominee director Bimpe Oke; 100% Apex Nominees Ltd; Suite 4B, Zuma Court. Not findable by name
  before the money trail reaches it, and the trail can't reach it before the h5 briefing; the Zuma Court
  address search lists it. C.A. = Commander Adaeze, never stated before the reveal.
- **Money, one direction.** Lagos (the beta's answer): wallet 0xE7…91A (a Lekki address) → 0.62 BTC
  (about ₦100M) → three OTC desks → the Ikeja POS floats (Odogwu Ventures among them) → cash home to Obi
  in Lekki. From Obi: the notebook's "C.A. — 2,500,000" every Friday is paid to the Ugbowo Relief
  Foundation as a "donation"; the Foundation pays C.A. Consulting a "retainer" of ₦10M on the 1st of
  every month since April 2019 (four of Obi's Fridays; about ₦910M in all). The route (the beta's answer:
  shrine → Asaba warehouse) is banked afterwards through Bluewater Logistics (a haulage front with no
  lorries; pays the drivers, ₦85,000 to Musa; hires its lorries from Sapele Haulage) into the Foundation
  (₦2.1bn in; its "staff" paid through Crestfield Payroll Services) and Serpentine Homes (Benin GRA
  houses). No route money flows to Obi or Lagos. Route nodes can't be traced before the route charge sheet.
  Obi's account (it quotes his notebook) joins the trail only once Lekki is behind you or the notebook is
  in hand.
- **Sapele Haulage Nig. Ltd** — RC 1611930, Plot 9 Ajogodo Road, Sapele; director Kunle Bamidele (also
  Bluewater's). It owns the lorry Musa drives and paid the welder (the beta's receipt). Musa never bought
  the lorry: "already there when I bought the truck" is CONTRADICTED by the lorry's registration (the M4
  checkpoint papers: "registered to SAPELE HAULAGE NIG. LTD") or the welder's receipt — either is proof.
- **Suite 4B, Zuma Court, Wuse II, Abuja** — one office behind Bluewater, Serpentine, the Foundation,
  C.A. Consulting, Apex Corporate Services (company secretary, Barr. Tamuno Briggs) and Silverline Media.
  No name search finds them before the case reaches them (Bluewater, Apex and Serpentine from Case 04, the
  Foundation once Lekki is behind you), so Case 02 and 03 can't put the cluster together.
- **The Engineer order (h5)** — he stays in play whatever the player chooses: h7 and the tail (t7) follow
  him. 'Pass it to a crime reporter': the story runs ("NACECA informant linked to kidnap ring") without his
  name, and the press learns nothing about his calls. 'Ask her about it': after the reveal she says she
  cut the Engineer off that night; her own "C." number still calls the ransom phone in the M7 tower data.
- **Evidence sources** — Osas hid the payroll drive in his shoe (M8). The Lekki cash and Obi's notebook
  were on the living-room coffee table; the laptop and the safe were in the study.
- **Mrs. C. Amadi** — a retired teacher in Uselu on the Foundation's trustee list. A decoy "C.A.", never her.
- **LND 590 XA** — a grey saloon with government plates: in the Lekki gatehouse log two nights before the
  raid, and at the Zuma Court handover (h5). That it is her office's pool car is said only at the trial (the
  motor-pool register) and after the reveal; before that, matching the two plates is the player's call.
- **The ledger** — at h4 the player may send it to her office by courier. Its custody gap ("Returned — page
  14 missing") and the court's "signed out by the Office of the Commander" exist only after the reveal.
- **Timeline** — day-scale (v12.2): the Week-1 briefing is at Lagos HQ the next morning; the h4–h7
  briefings are the hub's video call from Lagos (she is never in the room in Benin). Abuja work at h5 is

- **Tobi** — alive iff he was rescued, or in the chase branch when the hostage was not lost (Uche carried
  him out). The same rule drives the h6 lead, his statement, the case review and the epilogue. Only the
  deliberate rescue gives testimony at the trial; a chase-alive Tobi reads the drive for the prosecution
  but his doctors keep him out of the witness box.
- **Tunde** — if the player leans on informants and Tunde still talks to them, the Voice feeds Tunde the
  false Uselu tip (she says so in the reveal). Held on your charge sheet, or at Heat 70+, he won't talk.
- **Names** (v13 minor characters, chosen so no name echoes a canon character or a real person/brand):
  the Apex cover name is Obinna Anyanwu; defence counsel Chief Abiodun Fadeyi, SAN; the Apex referral is

## Season 2 — Operation Upstream (decided 20 Oct 2026)
These extend the canon above for the Season 2 layer (src/season2: Cases 09–15 with 13½, offices h9–h14); nothing
here changes Season 1. The full record, with the old-to-new tables, is docs/season2/CASES.md section 6.

- **The calendar.** Season 1's day one is Tue 6 Oct 2026, and Ekosodin falls about mid-October. Season 2 opens
  "six weeks later": Case 09 Wed 2 Dec 2026; Cases 10 and 11 on 3 and 4 Dec; Case 12 Thu 10 Dec; Cases 13 and 13½
  Fri 11 Dec (the second Friday); the pre-trial conference Fri 8 Jan 2027; the trial from Mon 11 Jan 2027, after the
  court's Christmas vacation; Case 15 in February 2027. In the fix pass every Season 2 date after 12 Sep 2026 moved
  +63 days (nine weeks, weekdays unchanged). The UPSTREAM ledger (Jun–Sep, last saved 12 Sep 2026) is older than
  Ekosodin and stays.
- **The drive.** Season 1's trial used only the payroll folder of exhibit 14, on certified printouts; Tobi read it
  for eleven days. The drive came back from the registry sealed. Nobody imaged the whole of it or opened its hidden
  `_old` folder, where the UPSTREAM sheet is, before Case 09.
- **C.A.'s second paymaster.** UPSTREAM's "C.A." line — ₦4.8m in cash on the 10th, paid by R.M.S. (Riverstone) —
  is Adaeze's second paymaster, beside the Foundation's ₦10M retainer to C.A. Consulting on the 1st. Season 1's money
  trail is unchanged; Season 2 says the two run side by side.
- **Tobi.** Alive iff rescued, or in the chase with the hostage not lost (`V13.tobiAlive()`); he testifies iff
