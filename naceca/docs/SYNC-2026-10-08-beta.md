# NACECA friends beta: wayfinding, deduction, paper UI · 8–9 Oct 2026

Against main @ 5dddbbe (v12.2 with the brand-sheet lockup). This pass comes from
playtest feedback: players couldn't find the mission or keep track of the suspect
in chases, the investigation was too easy, and the UI looked generic.
Story canon, characters and 3D models are unchanged. The only new content is
a handful of dead-end leads on the operations table and new consequence lines,
listed below.

## Build
- `build.py`: new `src/beta/` layer, appended after the v12 layer:
  `BETA_CSS = paper.css, wayfind.css, board.css, casework.css` (each a `<style id="beta-X">`),
  `BETA_JS = casework.js, wayfind.js, chase.js`. `beta/icons.js` is in `LOAD_ORDER`
  straight after `systems/util.js`, so every module can call `icon(name)`.
- `NACECA_OUT_DIR=<dir> python3 build.py` builds into another folder (for testing) and
  skips the music mirror.
- **The next drop must keep the `src/beta/` folder and these three lists in build.py.**
  A drop that replaces build.py without them silently removes the whole beta.

## 1. Wayfinding (`beta/wayfind.js`, `beta/chase.js`, `beta/wayfind.css`)
- HUD objective tracker, top left: objective text, then `· N m` to the target.
- An edge-of-screen arrow (line icon plus distance) when the target is off-screen,
  and a marker over it when it's on screen.
- Chases: a route ribbon on the ground from Kelechi to the suspect. It follows the
  scene's street lanes where they exist, otherwise it runs straight. There's also a red
  suspect blip on the minimap. After 3 s out of sight the blip becomes a "last seen" ping
  that refreshes every 4 s.
- Pace: KC 4.3 m/s with a 1.2 s opening dash, Ifeanyi 4.1 m/s; catch distance 1.9 m;
  0.5 s market stagger. The suspect escapes only after **20 s out of range** (gap over
  14 m), with a visible countdown. Touch players get a sprint button.
- Minimap: fixed transform so the map follows the player, plus blips.
- The meters and the route ribbon write to the DOM only when a value changes.
- Touch layout (this pass): portrait puts the chase meters full width above the
  thumbs; landscape puts them between the stick and the action cluster. The
  onboarding hint sits clear of the radio strip and the sprint button. The meters
  are placed by CSS (the old inline `placeMetersTouch` positioning is gone).

## 2. Deduction (`v12/v12_ops.js`, `v12_core.js`, `v12_night.js`, `beta/board.css`)
- **Dead-end leads** (`herring:true`): Lagos has Tunde, the UNKNOWN-BANK text, Grace
  Divine Cooperative and Ada. The Route has Alh. Rabiu Mukhtar, Delta Riverside
  Abattoir, the "levy" boy and Pa Eze. The Voice has Engr. Osaro, UNIBEN Hall 3 and
  Isihor Junction. Each appears when the player meets it. Grace Divine is now on the
  table once the market is done, so Lagos has 3 guaranteed dead ends before the
  charge sheet.
- **Filing:** you pencil links, then FILE them. The magistrate rules on each one:
  - a link that holds is **inked**;
  - a wrong one is **struck off**: a strike on the case and Integrity −2;
  - a dead end's own true fact (e.g. "Tunde works the market", "Osaro runs the mast")
    is **set aside** at no cost. Linking a dead end *into the crime* is still struck.
  - Three strikes and the warrant is refused until new evidence comes in: a new lead
    (a dead end turning up doesn't count), or a new link that holds. Follow the Money forgives the first wrong money link on
    each case.
  - The ruling sheet replaces the toast. Past three strikes the count reads "4 (warrant
    refused)", not "4/3".
- New true links (a seized item belongs to where it was seized): laptop–Lekki,
  notebook–Lekki, notebook–Ikeja POS (money), wallet–Lekki (money), Asaba SIMs–Asaba.
- **Warrant:** strength = 10 per inked link + 15 per theory (25 for the money trail)
  + 10 per opened key lead − 12 per strike. It signs at 60. The slip shows the stamp,
  the strength, the strikes and the need. The need text is always true: Senior sees
  "Needs stronger evidence"; Recruit is told whether to ink links, do fieldwork or
  wait for leads.
- **Fieldwork locks:** the phishing template needs a clean phone scan. KC's SIMs need
  KC's statement or the sleeve sweep. The Engineer needs Musa's tip or flip. The
  call records need a clean trace. New cards: the welder's receipt (m4) and Efe's
  statement (m7).
- **M2 table objective:** it completes on 3 inked links (including on a replay that
  already has them). On 3 strikes it closes as "refused at three strikes", and the van
  lets you go on.
- Senior hides Uche's hint panel, open theory questions, role tags until a card has
  ink, ink counts, analyst's notes and Night Shift highlights. Night Shift's failure
  text no longer names the link to file. The "more leads" line no longer gives a count
  (a count would show which new cards are dead ends).
- API (shared): `V12.caseOf`, `V12.warrantFor(c)` → `{strength,signed,refused,strikes,need}`,
  `V12.caseCards`, `V12.caseStrikes`, `V12.fileLinks`, `V12.pencil`, `V12.opsVisible`,
  `V12.opsRefresh`, `V12.caseLockedLeads`, `V12.m2TableCheck`. Save data: `S.game.ops`
  gains `struck`, `noted`, `cases`, `unlocked`, `v:2` (old saves migrate and keep their ink).

## 3. Charge sheets and difficulty (`beta/casework.js/.css`, settings, pause, docs, plan, hub, finale)
- **Difficulty:** Recruit (hints on) / Senior Agent (default). Pick it at NEW
  INVESTIGATION, or later in Settings › Casework. It's locked on the title screen and
  greyed out there. The old row is renamed "Action pace". API: `S.game.difficulty`,
  `gameDifficulty()`, `isRecruit()`, `body.cw-recruit` / `body.cw-senior`.
- Senior strips document hints: extra misses, underlines, red/green colouring and
  weight, "← MISMATCH", "(FAKE)", and "Right idea" feedback.
- **Charge sheet before each raid** (h2 → Lagos, h5 → the Route), plus WHO / METHOD /
  MONEY TRAIL / three pieces of evidence at the finale. It takes two taps and is
  permanent; replays keep it. Stored in `S.game.accusations[case]`.
- **Consequences** apply at the raid's aftermath:
  - Wrong suspect: Lagos Public Trust −8 and Agency −3; the Route Public Trust −6. Each
    also gets its own headline, phone message, news clip, board note, aftermath line and
    epilogue line.
  - Each wrong METHOD or MONEY line: that evidence is contested, plus **Agency −2 and
    grade −5** (new this pass). In Lagos, a wrong money line also strikes the +15
    money-trail intel.
  - All three right: Agency +3 and grade +5.
  - Wrong name at the finale: a public arrest, Public Trust −10 and Integrity −5.
  - Canon holds whatever you file: Obi is still arrested at Lekki, Ifeanyi is still the
    fixer, and the reveal always plays.
- Wrong-name lines follow the branches: KC escaped vs. caught, Musa jailed / tailed /
  flipped. A wrongly named Uche is arrested the next morning, not in the yard he is
  standing in. At the finale, a right motive or money trail argued against the wrong
  person is "SET ASIDE", never "HOLDS".
- **Warrant at filing:** if it's refused or unsigned, you either go back to the table
  or go in without a warrant. That costs Agency −5, contests the raid evidence and caps
  the grade at B. The M3 plan reads the warrant you filed, and its +2 credit is
  applied once.

## 4. Visual identity (styles.css, v12.css, `beta/paper.css`, `beta/icons.js`, index.html, …)
- Palette tokens on `:root`: `--manila #E9DCC0`, `--ink #1C1C1A`, `--green #0B6E4F`,
  `--red #B3261E`, `--grey #6B6B63`, plus contrast-checked shades. The legacy tokens
  are remapped onto the palette.
- No UI gradients, glows, backdrop blur or emoji. 56 line icons in `beta/icons.js`.
  The mini-game canvases, the street-talk silhouette and the share card are flat too
  (this pass). In-world art (3D textures, the car interior) is exempt.
- Oswald for HUD and headings, JetBrains Mono for case data. 1px rules, 2–4px radius.
  The operations table, case notes, documents, charge sheets, plan and aftermath look
  like manila paper with rubber stamps.
- Touch: keyboard hints hidden, every target ≥44px (the tune sliders included, this
  pass). Small text ≥4.5:1: no red text on ink, and red/green on paper is drawn as an
  ink word with a coloured underline. Phone contacts use palette discs.

## Harness (`tools/harness/`)
`node tools/harness/run.cjs tools/harness/scenarios/<name>.cjs [--html <abs path>] [--w 390 --h 844] [--touch 1] [--shots dir]`
- `three_stub.js` replaces three.js (cdnjs is blocked here), so nothing is drawn in 3D;
  all the DOM and state logic runs.
- Scenarios: `board_*`, `casework_*`, `wayfind_*`, `visual_touch` (390×844 and
  844×390 with `--touch 1`), `visual_desktop` (1280×800) and `board_shots` (both
  sizes). Files named `*_lib` or `_*` are helpers, not scenarios.
