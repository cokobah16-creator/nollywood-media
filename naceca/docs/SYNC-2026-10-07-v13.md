# NACECA v13 — the investigation layer ("The Case File") · 7 Oct 2026

Built on main @ f7baab8 (v12). Additive: a new layer in `src/v13/`, appended after the v12 layer by
`build.py`. No engine or v12 file is edited; every hook is a `V12.wrap` (or a wrap of a V12 function).
Delete `src/v13/` and its block in `build.py` and the game is v12 again.

## Files
- `src/v13/v13_data.js` — content: evidence model metadata, phones, money trail, CAC registry, statements,
  warrants, weekly briefings, gatekeeper dialogue (incl. Igbo), radio, court counts
- `src/v13/v13_intel.js` — the model: one record per exhibit (tier, source, custody, s.84, who knows),
  warrants, integrity, playstyle, mission hooks, finale extras, radio via `radioLine`
- `src/v13/v13_desk.js` — the Case Desk (Locker · Phones · Money · CAC · Statements · Warrants · Ops table ▸ · Review)
- `src/v13/v13_briefing.js` — the weekly briefing (2 of 3 leads), gatekeeper calls, surveillance van, Apex undercover
- `src/v13/v13_court.js` — FRN v. Cdr. Adaeze & 2 Ors, and the post-credits case review
- `src/v13/v13_boot.js` — all wiring (wraps)
- `src/v13/v13.css`
- `tools/v13_test.py` — end-to-end test of the layer through v12's real flows (19 checks)
- `tools/playthrough.py`, `tools/finale_test.py` — now play v11 field skills, v12 documents, the raid plan,
  the operations table and v12's accusation; the finale test also drives the trial and the review

## How it meets v12
- Warrants are applied for (Case Desk ▸ Warrants, the raid plan at the Lekki gate, the Week 4/5 briefings).
  `V12.warrant()` now means "the Lekki warrant was granted". Inked theories count double as grounds; the
  intel tier at 50 became MAGISTRATE (+2 on every basis). A refusal leaks: shorter wipe at Lekki, shorter
  cabinet window at Ugbowo, or the Ekosodin house reaching her desk.
- Weekly briefings run before Cases 04–08 (`loadMission` wrap). After Night Shift the Week 1 briefing
  comes before the "BENIN BYPASS · TWO DAYS LATER" card (`titleCard` wrap).
- Findings become evidence items (`inv_*`) that v12's accusation can use; `V12.strongAgainstAdaeze`
  counts C.A. Consulting (record + money), and the pool car at Lekki + Zuma Court (gatehouse + plate match).
- A proven or contested finale goes to trial before the epilogue (`startEpilogue` wrap); the verdict
  replaces Adaeze's epilogue line; the case review opens after the credits.
- Dropped in favour of v12: the v9 notebook (v12 has notes + the sealed accusation) and the cork-board
  additions (v12's operations table replaced the board). News bulletins play on v12's radio strip.
- Underworld Heat ≥ 70 locks Tunde out; ≥ 60 starts the Apex undercover under suspicion.
- HUD EVIDENCE opens the Case Desk (CASE FILE still opens the operations table); the operations table
  has a CASE DESK button; J opens it on desktop.
